# Quality Bar

## Universal Gates

| Gate | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Truth | Source values and meaning are unchanged; derived values are code-produced and traceable |
| Utility | The artifact supports a named reader job with a clear answer, evidence path, caveats, and action |
| Taste | The composition is content-specific, coherent, restrained, legible, and free of generic AI treatment |
| Surface | The real target surface has no placeholder, overflow, overlap, missing asset, unreadable text, or broken state |

All applicable gates must pass. Mechanical validation is a floor; real-surface visual inspection is mandatory.

## Assurance Lanes

### Express

Use only for reversible internal exploration with known direction, no source-backed or derived claims, no sensitive or untrusted input, no publication, and no consequential interaction. Require the universal gates and disclose omitted guarantees.

### Standard

Use for ordinary source-backed reports, dashboards, charts, posters, decks, and evidence boards. Add source identity, applicable summary or claim mapping, target-surface QA, and an independent content-and-visual review. Independent means a separate review pass; it does not require a separate agent unless requested.

### Assured

Use for derived values, sensitive or untrusted inputs, publication, formal decisions, consequential interaction, dependent multi-artifact output, or explicit high assurance. Add complete provenance, controlled execution, external evidence binding, and independent release review. See `advanced-assurance.md`.

Unknown material risk escalates. Lane selection changes assurance depth, never the truth or taste floor.

## Visual Review Rubric

- **Content fit**: shape and density match the material and reading mode.
- **Hierarchy**: answer, evidence, caveats, and sources have distinct priority.
- **Ownership**: the composition can be explained from this content, not a generic template.
- **Typography**: text is legible, wrapped, and sized for the target language and surface.
- **Color**: semantic, accessible, and subordinate to structure.
- **Media**: every image, icon, diagram, and motion element has a content job.
- **Responsiveness**: declared narrow states remain readable and non-overlapping.
- **Fallback**: static and reduced states preserve meaning.
- **Finish**: no placeholder, internal note, empty frame, broken asset, or accidental style remains.

## Evidence Status

Use monotonic labels:

1. `structure_passed`: required structure and applicable mechanical checks passed.
2. `evidence_traced`: visible claims and values are bound to sources or derived output.
3. `visually_reviewed`: the real target surface passed manual visual inspection.
4. `decision_ready`: required risk and release reviews passed for the declared use.
5. `user_validated`: observed user-task evidence exists; approval alone is insufficient.

Delivery reports `Lane`, `Proven`, `Not proven`, and `Next`. Do not translate a lower stage into a higher claim.
