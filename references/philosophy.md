# Aesthetic Philosophy

## Purpose

Aesthetic quality is not decoration. It is the disciplined alignment of content, hierarchy, medium, and attention so the reader sees the correct relationship quickly and remembers it accurately.

## Principles

1. **Content before style**: the composition follows the reader job and evidence shape, not a fashionable visual treatment.
2. **Hierarchy before ornament**: establish meaning with position, scale, spacing, grouping, typography, and contrast before adding effects.
3. **Truthful encoding**: visual position, length, area, color, motion, and sequence must not exaggerate magnitude or causality.
4. **Less but better**: every border, card, image, icon, control, and animation must earn its attention.
5. **Context-fit density**: dashboards favor stable scanning, reports favor reading rhythm, decks favor sequential contrast, and posters favor one decisive hierarchy.
6. **Platform humility**: HTML, PPTX, screenshots, dashboards, and print have different strengths. Preserve meaning across fallbacks and do not claim unsupported fidelity.

## Taste Contract

Before selecting a template, define:

- `visual_thesis`: the compositional point of view;
- `content_tension`: the contrast, hierarchy, change, or conflict that deserves first attention;
- `signature_move`: zero or one memorable visual action and its reader value;
- `anti_reference`: a concrete pattern this content must not resemble;
- `restraint_rule`: what is removed, repeated less, or kept quiet.

The contract predicts layout or evidence treatment. "Clean", "modern", "premium", a font, palette, library, or template id is not a thesis.

## Expression Ladder

| Level | Job | Admission rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `L0-static` | Complete claim, evidence, caveat, source, and reading order | Required for every artifact |
| `L1-interactive` | Inspect, compare, filter, configure, disclose, or act | Add only for a named reader task; no hover-only meaning |
| `L2-motion` | Explain state, direction, sequence, rhythm, emphasis, or feedback | Add only when materially clearer than the static state |

Every enhancement has a complete static and reduced-motion fallback. PPT uses an editable still, selected state, annotation, appendix, or ordered slide sequence.

## Attention Discipline

- One surface has exactly one primary attention region.
- One artifact has no more than one signature move.
- Ambient effects default to zero.
- Evidence, caveats, sources, and controls stay legible and visually subordinate, never hidden.
- Use type, spacing, position, grouping, and contrast before glow, blur, shadow, depth, or motion.

## Anti-AI Rules

Reject these defaults unless the content supplies a specific reason and a quieter alternative was considered:

- generic purple/cyan gradients, neon heroes, glass panels, glowing borders, particles, or decorative grids;
- card-per-paragraph layouts, nested cards, equal-weight bento grids, and excessive pills;
- oversized titles that displace evidence or tiny evidence that preserves drama;
- atmospheric filler images, fake product UI, fake charts, emoji icons, or decorative 3D objects;
- repeated fade-ups, looping backgrounds, parallax, tilt, glare, or cursor effects without semantic value;
- visually distinctive components from multiple libraries without normalized tokens, geometry, states, and motion.

## Design Review

The reviewer must answer: **Why does this composition belong to this content?**

A passing answer cites the reader job, visual thesis, content tension, evidence, signature move, and restraint rule. Validators can reject known defects; they cannot prove taste, content ownership, or reader success.
