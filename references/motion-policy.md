# Motion Policy

## Purpose

Motion supports state, direction, and rhythm. It must not be used as decoration or as the only carrier of meaning.

## Rules

- Every motion recipe must have a static or reduced-motion fallback.
- Deck motion should be semantic: cover reveal, evidence cascade, directional transition, metric emphasis, or close.
- Do not use one generic fade-up on every slide.
- Motion should not hide source notes, labels, caveats, or evidence.
- If browser validation cannot run, do not claim motion QA.

## Reduced Motion

Use `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` to remove transform-based motion and keep content visible.
